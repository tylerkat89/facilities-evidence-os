# Facilities Evidence OS

Interact with your building, not your spreadsheets.

Map-first facilities record. Issues, vendors, evidence, inventory, and budgets stay on the room they belong to. Enter it once.

## What’s in here

- `public/feos.html` — the floor (demo + blank template)
- `public/card.html` — QR landing / founding pilot
- `public/qr.html` — hold-up QR (`/qr`, roommate `/qr/ups`)
- `src/` + `server/` + `migrations/` — pilot leads, demo gate, budget RBAC

## Not in this repo

Secrets. Database URLs. Customer evidence. Those stay off GitHub.
